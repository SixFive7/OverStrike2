# gg_vinceville

A village of small houses on a big grassy field, generated by script (`generate.py`); Workshop item 3831242232 ([the maps](../README.md)).
The map was written from scratch for build 4, and build 5 fixes what the first look at it in a game client found ([Builds](#builds)).

## What it builds

- **The field.** 2560 x 2560 units of play inside a player clip, gently rolling (outside the ponds the ground stays within about 20
  units of level), with dirt paths across it.
  - The ground is one height field of 64-unit cells, 3584 x 3584 with the forest round the field, cut into 49 chunks of 8 x 8 cells.
    Each chunk is a closed mesh that shares every edge vertex with its neighbours, and nothing is ever cut out of the ground.
  - Each building stands on a flat pad at its own level that reaches 128 beyond its base; the field blends into the pad over 160.
- **Eight buildings**, each one closed mesh: the union of axis-aligned boxes on the building's own grid (base, walls, floors and steps,
  less the doors, windows and stair holes), of which only the outer surface is written. Walls, floors and stairs meet with no seam,
  no face inside another and no T-junction. Each roof is a solid prism whose underside is 2 below the wall tops.

  | Building | Size (east-west x north-south) | Walls, roof | Inside |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | H1 farmhouse | 448 x 320 | brick, tiles | two storeys, 12 steps up along the north wall |
  | H2 cottage | 256 x 320 | stucco, thatch | one room |
  | H3 green house | 384 x 256 | green plaster, metal | one room |
  | H4 stone house | 288 x 320 | stone, tiles | one room |
  | H5 red house | 384 x 288 | red plaster, tiles | two storeys, 12 steps up along the north wall |
  | B1 barn | 512 x 320 | planks, corrugated metal | 232 tall; doors 160 x 168 south, 128 x 168 north at the east end, clear of the stair, and an ordinary one east; a hay loft over the west end, 11 steps up along the north wall |
  | H6 small house | 256 x 224 | brick, tiles | one room |
  | S1 shed | 224 x 160 | planks, corrugated metal | one room, a door at each end |

  Every building has at least two doors (72 x 104 but for the barn's). Windows are open, 60 tall from 34 over the floor: a crouching
  player gets through. Walls are 8 thick, a storey is 144 floor to floor, and a step is 12 up and 16 deep. Roofs pitch at 47 degrees,
  too steep to stand on, and overhang 24 at the eaves and 12 at the gables.
- **The stair holes.** A player is a box 32 wide, so the hole in an upper floor over its stair (`stair_hole`) starts a box's width
  and 8 before the first tread with less than 80 under the floor, and reaches a box's width and 8 past the stair's open side: a box
  standing anywhere on the stair, or rising onto its next step, has the floor's underside above its head with room to spare. The
  ground floors under H1's and H5's upper floors have dark wooden ceilings, like the roofs' undersides.
- **Two ponds** (radius 200 and 180): bowls in the ground with shallow water in them, 12 to 14 deep in the middle, as `func_water` in
  four convex pieces each. The mud round the water meets the grass along the ground's triangles; 74 tufts of tall grass stand on that
  edge to break its straight lines.
- **Cover in the open.**
  - 7 low dry-stone walls (40 high), one of them with a gap where a path crosses, and 10 haystacks (4 of them two high), as world
    meshes reaching into the ground.
  - Solid props: three cars, a van, two crates, two barrels and a wheelbarrow, each at least 24 from the middle of every path.
  - Foliage, with no collision (players and bullets pass through it): 29 trees, 70 bushes and 208 tufts of grass and flowers in the
    field; 159 trees and 101 foliage cards in the forest round it, outside the clip.
- **Spawns.**
  - 24 `info_deathmatch_spawn`: one inside each building and 16 out in the field.
  - 10 `info_player_terrorist` in the west (3 of them inside H1, B1 and H6) and 10 `info_player_counterterrorist` in the east (3
    inside H3, H4 and H5).
  - Each is picked farthest-first among the points where a player stands clear, at least 96 from the others. Outside it faces the
    middle of the field, inside the middle of its room (`spawn_yaw`), turned to the most open way within 90 degrees when something
    solid stands closer than 128 that way. The entity is 8 above the floor.
- **Lighting.**
  - Overpass's sky (`sky_de_overpass_01.vmat`) and a stationary `light_environment`, 50 degrees up.
  - One `light_omni2` per room, 1600 lm: 11. It hangs 20 under a roof and 40 under an upper floor.
  - Nine `env_combined_light_probe_volume`s, one over the field and a finer one per building.
  - A master `post_processing_volume` allows auto exposure from 0.8 to 2.0.
  - A lightmap resolution volume round the play area; the lightmaps bake at 1024, quality 0 (fast).
- **Containment.** A player clip 16 thick at the edge of the play area, from under the ground to the sky. Everything is inside a
  sealed `toolsskybox` shell of 3584 x 3584 x 1536.

Only stock CS2 materials and models are used, by reference, so the addon has no materials of its own.

## Files

- `generate.py` writes the map source into the Workshop Tools' content folder (`--addon` names another addon folder; the map keeps
  its name):
  - `content/csgo_addons/<addon>/maps/gg_vinceville.vmap`, keyvalues2 DMX, the element structure Hammer saves: one `CMapMesh` per
    ground chunk, building, roof, wall, haystack and tool box, and the water as `func_water`;
  - `game/csgo_addons/<addon>/addoninfo.txt`;
  - and `layout.json` (`--layout`): every mesh with its vertices, faces and materials, the props, the ponds, every building's floors
    with their holes, its steps and doors, and the spawns with the way they face.
- `checks.py` checks the world on `layout.json`, on the meshes themselves (its docstring says how):
  1. every mesh and piece of water is closed, each edge used once each way;
  2. no two drawn faces lie in one plane over each other (z-fighting, or two faces back to back);
  3. a vertical ray every 16 units meets the ground's top exactly once, over the whole terrain;
  4. from the sky, a ray every 16 units over the play area first meets a face that looks up;
  5. on every floor of every building, outside its stair holes, a ray every 8 units first meets that floor at its own height, and
     every step's tread is there;
  6. every spawn stands on a floor or the ground, in a player's box that is inside nothing, out of the water and apart from the
     others, and faces 96 or more of open space at eye height; and there are enough for every mode;
  7. every path's middle is 24 or more from every solid prop, stone wall and haystack.
- `walkcheck.py`, on the same layout, walks a player's box (32 x 32, 72 tall standing, 54 crouched, steps of 18 that it rises into
  before it steps over, 2 to spare over its head):
  1. 80 clear over the middle of every step;
  2. up and down every staircase, standing and crouched, along lines across its whole width, from hard against the wall to
     overhanging the open side by 15;
  3. straight in through every door along lines across its whole clear width, from 48 outside to 96 inside;
  4. from the field to every spot of every floor, every step and every spawn.
- `raycast.py` is the vertical ray caster both checks look with.
- `build.py` runs `generate.py`, then compiles the map with `maps/build_map.py`.
- `preview.py` renders the Workshop preview (1280 x 720 JPEG) from `layout.json` with `maps/previewkit.py`, on the CPU.
- `workshop.json`: the item's title, description and tags, and the addon folder its build is in (`gg_vinceville_v5`);
  `workshop_id.txt`: the item.

## Rebuild

```
python maps\gg_vinceville\build.py --addon gg_vinceville_v5 --layout <scratch>\layout.json --log <scratch>\compile.log
python -I maps\gg_vinceville\checks.py <scratch>\layout.json
python -I maps\gg_vinceville\walkcheck.py <scratch>\layout.json
python maps\gg_vinceville\preview.py <scratch>\layout.json <scratch>\preview.jpg
```

`python -I maps\selfcheck.py` runs the generator, both checks and the preview without CS2. The map compile build 5 shipped with
(17 s, with the GPU bake):

```
resourcecompiler.exe -threads 4 -fshallow -unbufferedio -i "<CS2>\content\csgo_addons\gg_vinceville_v5\maps\gg_vinceville.vmap" -noassert -world -phys -breakpad -nop4 -retail -bakelighting -lightmapMaxResolution 1024 -lightmapVRadQuality 0
```

**The Workshop upload is the addon folder** `game\csgo_addons\gg_vinceville_v5`: `addoninfo.txt` and `maps\gg_vinceville.vpk`
(5.8 MB).

## Checks (build 5)

- **The compile:** 73 compiled, 0 failed. The log's only remarks: the usual `scripts/detail_prop_types.vdata_c` not found and
  Fossilize's pipeline-cache notice. The map source it compiled is what `generate.py` writes, byte for byte.
- **The map's VPK:** 74 entries, every CRC as recorded: the world, its physics, 52 world nodes, the lightmaps and the light probe
  atlas, and the entity lump with 9 entity models (the 8 pieces of water and the exposure volume). The source holds 24 deathmatch,
  10 T and 10 CT spawns, 576 props, 11 lights and 9 probe volumes.
- **checks.py**, on the layout the compiled source was written with: 105 meshes and pieces of water closed; 16,413 drawn faces in
  4,520 planes, none over another; 49,729 ground rays, 25,600 rays from the sky and 14,852 floor rays as they should be; 44 spawns
  standing clear and facing open space; 688 points along 4 paths, each clear of every obstacle.
- **walkcheck.py:** 35 steps with 80 or more clear over their middle; 192 walks up and down the 3 staircases and 223 walks in through
  the 17 doors, none stuck; 96,607 of 97,132 standing spots reached on foot, among them all 11,594 spots on the 11 floors, all 35
  steps and all 44 spawns. The 525 others are the tops of the stone walls and haystacks, which take a jump.
- **The checks catch what they are for.** Build 4's own layout fails `walkcheck.py`: 64 of 192 stair walks stuck in H1 and H5,
  standing and crouched, and 2,776 spots and 2 spawns not reached. Build 4's defects planted into this generator, one at a time,
  each fail their check: its stair holes (64 stair walks stuck), its barn's north door over the stair's foot (24 door walks stuck),
  its spawns all facing the middle of the field (5 facing a solid within 96, H3's inside spawn 40 from its west wall), and its van,
  cars and wall on the paths (78 path points too near). So does each defect planted in a copy of the layout: a ground triangle taken
  out (closed, ground), a face laid over another (overlaps), a floor tile taken out (closed, floors), a spawn moved into a wall
  (spawns), a house's doors walled up (walkcheck: its floor and spawns not reached) and a beam over a stair (walkcheck: headroom).

## In game

The checks read the map source; the compiled map is first seen in a game client from the Workshop. Things to look at there:

- **The ground:** walk the edge of the field and the paths across it, and look along the ground from the top of a stacked haystack:
  no holes, no seams, no flicker.
- **Every house:** in through each door, round each room, out of a window crouching; the walls' corners, the floors where they meet
  the walls, and the ceilings, for gaps and flickering faces.
- **The stairs:** up and down in H1 and H5 and onto the barn's loft, walking, standing and crouched, along the wall and along the
  open edge; nothing to bump your head on.
- **The ponds:** walk through both; the water's surface and the bank.
- **The roofs:** from outside, the eaves and the gable ends where they meet the walls.
- **Spawns:** many respawns, never in a wall, a roof or the water, and never facing a wall up close.

## Known gaps

- **No vis.** The map draws whole, as every published build has.
- **No cubemaps baked.** CS2 bakes cubemaps in Hammer or in the running game, and neither was used; the probe volumes carry the diffuse
  light, and the ponds' water reflects nothing.
- **The mud's edge round the ponds** follows the ground's triangles. The tufts of grass break it up; a soft edge takes a blend
  material painted per vertex, which no build here has tried.
- **Foliage has no collision**, so trees do not stop bullets or players.
- **Shallow ponds**, no swimming, and no sewer: the builds before 4 had deep ponds and a sewer; this map has neither.
- **The light balance** - 1600 lm per room and the 0.8-2.0 exposure range - was looked at in a game client in build 4 only.
