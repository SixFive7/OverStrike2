# gg_vinceville

A village of small houses on a big grassy field, generated by script (`generate.py`); Workshop item 3831242232 ([the maps](../README.md)).
Build 4 is a new map, written from scratch: the builds before it had holes in the ground and faces lying over each other, and none
of their geometry is left.

## What it builds

- **The field.** 2560 x 2560 units of play inside a player clip, gently rolling (outside the ponds the ground stays within about 20
  units of level), with dirt paths across it.
  - The ground is one height field of 64-unit cells, 3584 x 3584 with the forest round the field, cut into 49 chunks of 8 x 8 cells.
    Each chunk is a closed mesh that shares every edge vertex with its neighbours, and nothing is ever cut out of the ground.
  - Each building stands on a flat pad at its own level that reaches 128 beyond its base; the field blends into the pad over 160.
- **Eight buildings**, each one closed mesh: the union of axis-aligned boxes on the building's own grid (base, walls, floors and steps,
  less the doors, windows and stair openings), of which only the outer surface is written. Walls, floors and stairs meet with no seam,
  no face inside another and no T-junction. Each roof is a solid prism whose underside is 2 below the wall tops.

  | Building | Size (east-west x north-south) | Walls, roof | Inside |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | H1 farmhouse | 448 x 320 | brick, tiles | two storeys, 12 steps up along the north wall |
  | H2 cottage | 256 x 320 | stucco, thatch | one room |
  | H3 green house | 384 x 256 | green plaster, metal | one room |
  | H4 stone house | 288 x 320 | stone, tiles | one room |
  | H5 red house | 384 x 288 | red plaster, tiles | two storeys, 12 steps up along the north wall |
  | B1 barn | 512 x 320 | planks, corrugated metal | 232 tall, doors 160 x 168 north and south and an ordinary one east; a hay loft over the west end, 11 steps up along the north wall |
  | H6 small house | 256 x 224 | brick, tiles | one room |
  | S1 shed | 224 x 160 | planks, corrugated metal | one room, a door at each end |

  Every building has at least two doors (72 x 104). Windows are open, 60 tall from 34 over the floor: a crouching player gets
  through. Walls are 8 thick, a storey is 144 floor to floor, and a step is 12 up and 16 deep with at least 80 clear over it. Roofs
  pitch at 47 degrees, too steep to stand on, and overhang 24 at the eaves and 12 at the gables.
- **Two ponds** (radius 200 and 180): bowls in the ground with shallow water in them, 12 to 14 deep in the middle, as `func_water` in
  four convex pieces each.
- **Cover in the open.**
  - 7 low dry-stone walls (40 high) and 10 haystacks (4 of them two high), as world meshes reaching into the ground.
  - Solid props: three cars, a van, two crates, two barrels and a wheelbarrow.
  - Foliage, with no collision (players and bullets pass through it): 29 trees, 70 bushes and 134 tufts of grass and flowers in the
    field; 159 trees and 101 foliage cards in the forest round it, outside the clip.
- **Spawns.**
  - 24 `info_deathmatch_spawn`, for Arms Race's `mp_randomspawn 1`: one inside each building and 16 out in the field.
  - 10 `info_player_terrorist` in the west (3 of them inside H1, B1 and H6) and 10 `info_player_counterterrorist` in the east (3
    inside H3, H4 and H5), for a mode with team spawns.
  - Each is picked farthest-first among the points where a player stands clear, at least 96 from the others, and faces the middle of
    the field. The entity is 8 above the floor.
- **Lighting.**
  - Overpass's sky (`sky_de_overpass_01.vmat`) and a stationary `light_environment`, 50 degrees up.
  - One `light_omni2` under each ceiling: 11.
  - Nine `env_combined_light_probe_volume`s, one over the field and a finer one per building.
  - A master `post_processing_volume` allows auto exposure from 0.8 to 2.0.
  - A lightmap resolution volume round the play area; the lightmaps bake at 1024, quality 0 (fast).
- **Containment.** A player clip 16 thick at the edge of the play area, from under the ground to the sky. Everything is inside a
  sealed `toolsskybox` shell of 3584 x 3584 x 1536.

Only stock CS2 materials and models are used, by reference, so the addon has no materials of its own.

## Files

- `generate.py` writes the map source into the Workshop Tools' content folder (`--addon` names another addon folder; the map keeps
  its name):
  - `content/csgo_addons/<addon>/maps/gg_vinceville.vmap`, keyvalues2 DMX, the element structure Hammer saves: one `CMapMesh` per
    ground chunk, building, roof, wall, haystack and tool box, and the water as `func_water`;
  - `game/csgo_addons/<addon>/addoninfo.txt`;
  - and `layout.json` (`--layout`): every mesh with its vertices, faces and materials, the props, the ponds, every building's floors
    and steps, and the spawns.
- `checks.py` checks the world on `layout.json`, on the meshes themselves (its docstring says how):
  1. every mesh and piece of water is closed, each edge used once each way;
  2. no two drawn faces lie in one plane over each other (z-fighting, or two faces back to back);
  3. a vertical ray every 16 units meets the ground's top exactly once, over the whole terrain;
  4. from the sky, a ray every 16 units over the play area first meets a face that looks up;
  5. on every floor of every building, a ray every 8 units first meets that floor, and every step's tread is there;
  6. every spawn stands on a floor or the ground, in a player's box that is inside nothing, out of the water and apart from the others,
     and there are enough for every mode.
- `walkcheck.py`, on the same layout, walking only (no jumps, no crouching): headroom of 80 over every step, and a player walking from
  the field reaches every spot of every floor, every step and every spawn.
- `raycast.py` is the vertical ray caster both checks look with.
- `build.py` runs `generate.py`, then compiles the map with `maps/build_map.py`.
- `preview.py` renders the Workshop preview (1280 x 720 JPEG) from `layout.json` with `maps/previewkit.py`, on the CPU.
- `workshop.json`: the item's title, description and tags, and the addon folder its build is in (`gg_vinceville_v4`);
  `workshop_id.txt`: the item.

## Rebuild

```
python maps\gg_vinceville\build.py --addon gg_vinceville_v4 --layout <scratch>\layout.json --log <scratch>\compile.log
python -I maps\gg_vinceville\checks.py <scratch>\layout.json
python -I maps\gg_vinceville\walkcheck.py <scratch>\layout.json
python maps\gg_vinceville\preview.py <scratch>\layout.json <scratch>\preview.jpg
```

`python -I maps\selfcheck.py` runs the generator, both checks and the preview without CS2. The map compile build 4 shipped with
(32 s, of which the GPU bake is about 20 s):

```
resourcecompiler.exe -threads 4 -fshallow -unbufferedio -i "<CS2>\content\csgo_addons\gg_vinceville_v4\maps\gg_vinceville.vmap" -noassert -world -phys -breakpad -nop4 -retail -bakelighting -lightmapMaxResolution 1024 -lightmapVRadQuality 0
```

**The Workshop upload is the addon folder** `game\csgo_addons\gg_vinceville_v4`: `addoninfo.txt` and `maps\gg_vinceville.vpk`
(5.8 MB).

## Checks (build 4)

- **The compile:** 76 compiled, 0 failed. The log's only remarks: the usual `scripts/detail_prop_types.vdata_c` not found and
  Fossilize's pipeline-cache notice.
- **The map's VPK:** 77 entries, every CRC as recorded: the world, its physics, 55 world nodes, the lightmaps and the light probe
  atlas, and the entity lump with 9 entity models (the 8 pieces of water and the exposure volume). The source it was compiled from
  holds 24 deathmatch, 10 T and 10 CT spawns, 502 props, 11 lights and 9 probe volumes.
- **checks.py**, on the layout the compiled source was written with: 104 meshes and pieces of water closed; 16,001 drawn faces in
  4,520 planes, none over another; 49,729 ground rays, 25,600 rays from the sky and 15,062 floor rays as they should be; 44 spawns
  standing clear.
- **walkcheck.py:** 35 steps, each with 80 or more clear over it; 96,761 of 97,322 standing spots reached on foot, among them all
  11,816 spots on the 11 floors, all 35 steps and all 44 spawns. The 561 others are the tops of the stone walls and haystacks, which
  take a jump.
- **The checks catch what they are for:** each defect planted in a copy of the layout failed its check - a ground triangle taken out
  (closed, ground), a face laid over another (overlaps), a floor tile taken out (closed, floors), a spawn moved into a wall (spawns),
  a house's doors walled up (walkcheck: its floor and spawns not reached) and a beam over a stair (walkcheck: headroom).

## In game

The checks read the map source; the compiled map is first seen in a game client from the Workshop. Things to look at there:

- **The ground:** walk the edge of the field and the paths across it, and look along the ground from the top of a stacked haystack:
  no holes, no seams, no flicker.
- **Every house:** in through each door, round each room, out of a window crouching; the walls' corners, the floors where they meet
  the walls, and the ceilings, for gaps and flickering faces.
- **The stairs:** up and down in H1 and H5 and onto the barn's loft, walking, without jumping; nothing to bump your head on.
- **The ponds:** walk through both; the water's surface and the bank.
- **The roofs:** from outside, the eaves and the gable ends where they meet the walls.
- **Spawns:** with `mp_randomspawn 1`, many respawns, never in a wall, a roof or the water; in a team mode, T in the west and CT in the
  east.

## Known gaps

- **No vis.** The map draws whole, as every published build has.
- **No cubemaps baked.** CS2 bakes cubemaps in Hammer or in the running game, and neither was used; the probe volumes carry the diffuse
  light.
- **Foliage has no collision**, so trees do not stop bullets or players.
- **Shallow ponds**, no swimming, and no sewer: the earlier builds' deep ponds and sewer are not in this one.
- **The light balance** - 1600 lm per room and the 0.8-2.0 exposure range - had not been seen in a game client when build 4 shipped.
