# gg_moonglass

A moon base under a faceted glass dome, with glass almost everywhere, generated by script (`generate.py`); Workshop item 3831231522
([the maps](../README.md)). v2 is a content update of the same item under the same map name: less brightness and more glass than v1.

The theme is the generic one of Lunacy (Workshop 3070560242, another author's port of CS:GO's ar_lunacy), taken from its public
preview only: a moon base under a geodesic dome, grey ground, white modules and tanks, a greenhouse dome, pipes, a curved wall and Earth
in a black sky. Nothing else was taken - none of its files, its layout or its measurements. Everything here is generated by `generate.py`.

## What it builds (v2)

- **The dome.** An octahedron-based geodesic dome of frequency 11, 484 flat triangular panes, 1056 units in radius and flattened to 0.62,
  so the apex is at z 815. It stands on a concrete ring wall (radius 1024-1088, 160 high).
  - A steel strut runs along every edge inside the glass.
  - A player clip shell 12-20 units outside the dome keeps everyone in, whether the dome is broken or not.
  - A sealed `toolsskybox` box of 2304 x 2304 x 1632 encloses everything.
  - The sky is black. Earth hangs to the north-east: a self-lit faceted sphere with a generated texture, casting no shadow.
- **The base.**
  - **The hub:** a two-storey glass octagon in the centre. On the ground floor glass screens stand round the console; upstairs glass walls
    divide the storey into four rooms. The roof deck at z 384 is a solid core with a glass-floor ring round it, and on the core stands a
    glass pavilion with a frosted pyramid roof.
  - **Four tunnels**, double-skinned glass, each skin in two rows, run along the axes to four modules: north a greenhouse dome (frequency
    6); east the lab, with two glass partitions; south the habitat, its windows in split panes; west the pump room, with a thick glass
    screen, white tanks and pipes.
  - **The ring catwalk** runs at z 192 along the wall, with glass floor segments and a glass rail; four ramps lead up to it. Below it, 24
    radial glass stalls run from the ring's posts out to the wall.
  - **Glass bridges at z 192:** four, along the diagonals, from the ring to the hub's upper storey.
  - **The upper level at z 384:** two elevated ramps from the ring, over the greenhouse and the habitat, reach z 384. An octagonal glass
    ring there (floor and rails, round the hub) joins them, and two short sky bridges link it to the hub's roof.
  - **The open ground between the modules:** glass mini-mazes (two-row walls on a polar grid, mixing frosted, smoked, thick and clear),
    four glass booths with frosted or smoked roofs, and three climbable glass kiosks - two crates step up to a metal roof at z 128, which
    has a glass rail.
- **1,162 panes of glass** (v1 had 511), every one a `func_breakable` with material Glass:

  | Material | Panes | Health | Thickness | Surface | Look |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `glass_clear` | 403 | 1 | 4 | `glass` | light blue, see-through |
  | `glass_tint` | 299 | 15 | 6 | `glass` | dark smoked |
  | `glass_frost` | 189 | 60 | 10 | `plastic_solid` | frosted, close to opaque |
  | `glass_thick` | 139 | 30 | 14 | `glass` | thick blue-grey, see-through |
  | `glass_floor` | 60 | 5 | 8 | `glassfloor` | green-cyan, for floors |
  | `glass_green` | 72 | 1 | 4 | `glass` | the greenhouse |

  By place: the dome 484, the mazes 124, the greenhouse 96, the tunnels 96, the bridges at z 192 66, the ring at z 384 64, the hub 62,
  the stalls under the ring 48, the kiosks 33, the ring catwalk 28, the habitat 24, the booths 16, the lab 12, the pump room 9.
- **Bullet penetration.** CS2 sets it per surface property, in the game's own `scripts/surfaceproperties_game.txt`: `glass` and
  `glassfloor` both have `bulletPenetrationDistanceModifier 0.99`, and a map cannot add a surface property of its own. So the frosted panes
  use the stock `plastic_solid` (0.0): bullets should not pass until the pane breaks - inferred from the file, not seen in game.
- **Spawns.** 24 `info_deathmatch_spawn`, 16 on the ground and 8 at z 192 (the ring catwalk and the hub's upper storey), and 8 T (the
  south half) and 8 CT (the north half). Every spawn stands on solid floor, never on breakable glass and never under it, the big dome
  excepted, since it covers everything. The worst fall is from z 384, about 46 damage; there are no pits.
- **Lighting, v2.** Every source about 30% lower than v1:

  | Source | v1 | v2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Sun (stationary, south-west, 38 degrees) | 3.0 | 2.1 |
  | Sky ambient | 0.7 | 0.5 |
  | 22 base lamps | 2,200 lm | 1,500 lm |
  | 16 ring lamps | 1,600 lm | 1,100 lm |
  | Earth's self-illumination | 1.6 | 1.25 |

  - **Exposure:** the master `post_processing_volume` holds auto exposure between 0.65 and 1.6 (v1: 0.7-2.0). The lower minimum lets bright
    sunlit views settle darker; the lower maximum stops dark interiors being pushed up.
  - **Darker materials:** the white panels went from 86% to 74% white, the regolith is about 12% darker, and every glass tint a little darker.
  - **The bake:** two `env_combined_light_probe_volume`s; lightmaps at 1024, quality 0. The baked lightmaps came out about 31% darker on
    average than v1's, and the light probes about 32% darker; none of the probe slices is black.
- **Size.** 2,386 m² walkable as `sizes.json` measures it, class M (v1: 2,644). Glass floors do not count.

## Files

- `generate.py` writes the map source, the materials and their generated textures into the Workshop Tools' content folder, and
  `layout.json` (`--layout`). It needs numpy and Pillow.
- `build.py` runs `generate.py`, then compiles the materials and the map at below normal priority with 4 threads. It waits until no other
  `resourcecompiler.exe` runs before each compile; it clears old compiled materials first, because constant-colour textures carry their
  colour's hash in their name and stale ones would ride along in the upload; afterwards it moves vrad3's working files out of the addon
  folder.
- `preview.py` renders the Workshop preview from `layout.json`, on the CPU.

## Rebuild

```
python maps\gg_moonglass\build.py --novis --layout <scratch>\layout.json --log <scratch>\compile.log
python maps\gg_moonglass\preview.py <scratch>\layout.json <scratch>\preview.jpg --earth "<CS2>\content\csgo_addons\gg_moonglass\materials\gg_moonglass\earth_color.png"
```

The map compile of v2 (30 s, the GPU bake included):

```
resourcecompiler.exe -threads 4 -fshallow -unbufferedio -i "<CS2>\content\csgo_addons\gg_moonglass\maps\gg_moonglass.vmap" -noassert -world -phys -breakpad -nop4 -retail -bakelighting -lightmapMaxResolution 1024 -lightmapVRadQuality 0
```

**The Workshop upload is the whole addon folder** `game\csgo_addons\gg_moonglass`: `maps\gg_moonglass.vpk` and `materials\gg_moonglass\`.

## Checks (v2)

- **The compile:** 1,184 compiled, 0 failed, 1 skipped; the log's only remarks are the usual harmless ones.
- **The entity lump**, decompiled: 1,162 `func_breakable`, all `material 0`, with 1,162 distinct models; 40 spawns, none on or under
  breakable glass but for the dome; 38 lights and 2 probe volumes.
- **The size measure:** all 40 spawns standable, a walkable area of 2,386 m², and the clip shell unchanged.

## Known gaps

- **No vis.** On v1 `-vis` ran 12 minutes without finishing, and ignored `-threads 4` (it used about 17 cores). v2 has more geometry, so it
  was not tried.
- **No cubemaps baked.** CS2 bakes them in Hammer or in the running game, and neither was used.
- **Not seen in a game client** when v2 shipped: the new light balance and exposure range, the tints and `plastic_solid`'s penetration.
- **Broken glass stays broken** until the map reloads.
