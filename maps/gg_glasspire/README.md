# gg_glasspire

A square glass tower around an open atrium, generated by script (`generate.py`); Workshop item 3831189574 ([the maps](../README.md)).

## What it builds

- **The tower.** 1280 x 1280 units around an open atrium of 512 x 512, with floors at z 0, 192, 384 and 576 and an open roof deck at 768.
  - Each floor's facade has its own colour band: grey, blue, green and yellow, with an orange parapet on the roof.
  - Four ramps of 26.6 degrees (192 up over 384) run along the outer walls: south, east, north, then west. Climbing them spirals up the
    tower. A half wall guards each ramp's hole on the floor above.
  - Cover on every floor: 8 columns, plus crates and half walls rotated 90 degrees per floor. The ground floor has a planter in the
    atrium; the roof has a machine room and air handlers.
- **292 panes of glass.** Every pane is a `func_breakable` with material Glass and health 1, so one bullet breaks it. No pane is larger
  than 128 units.

  | Glass | Panes |
  |---|---|
  | Outer windows (two per bay, every floor) | 160 |
  | Railings round the atrium (floors 1-3) | 48 |
  | Bridge floors (north-south on 1 and 3, east-west on 2) | 12 |
  | Bridge rails | 24 |
  | Floor patches (two 2 x 2 patches per upper floor and roof) | 32 |
  | Skylight over the atrium | 16 |

  The glass uses two tinted materials of the addon's own (`materials/gg_glasspire/`, `csgo_glass.vfx` with constant colours): light cyan
  for the windows and rails, a greener, frostier one for the glass you stand on. Steel beams stay when the glass breaks: under the
  bridges, under the patches' seams and under the skylight's grid.
- **Containment.** A player clip stands just outside the facade, from the ground up to z 1536, so broken windows and the roof edge keep
  everyone in. Everything sits inside a sealed `toolsskybox` shell of 3200 x 3200 x 1856. Outside there is a plaza and eight plain blocks
  of city, decoration only.
- **Spawns, never on glass.** 30 `info_deathmatch_spawn`, picked farthest-first on concrete over all five levels, for the Arms Race cfg's
  `mp_randomspawn 1`; also 10 `info_player_terrorist` (the south half) and 10 `info_player_counterterrorist` (the north half).
- **Lighting.**
  - The sky is Vertigo's (`sky_de_vertigo.vmat`); the sun a stationary `light_environment`.
  - Twelve static `light_omni2` hang under each ceiling, and four more light the atrium floor.
  - Five `env_combined_light_probe_volume`s, one per floor band and the roof, give players and weapons their light.
  - A master `post_processing_volume` allows auto exposure from 0.8 to 2.0.
  - The lightmaps bake at 1024, quality 0 (fast).
- **Size.** 3,725 m² walkable as `sizes.json` measures it, class L. Only concrete counts, because breakables are not world collision; with
  the glass floors it is about 4,360 m².

## Files

- `generate.py` writes the map source into the Workshop Tools' content folder:
  - `content/csgo_addons/gg_glasspire/maps/gg_glasspire.vmap`, keyvalues2 DMX, the element structure Hammer saves: one closed
    `CMapMesh` per box or wedge, and one `func_breakable` with one mesh child per pane;
  - the two glass `.vmat` files;
  - `game/csgo_addons/gg_glasspire/addoninfo.txt`;
  - and `layout.json` (`--layout`), with every piece, pane, spawn and light.
- `build.py` runs `generate.py`, compiles the two glass materials, then compiles the map, at below normal priority with 4 threads, so a
  game running on the same PC keeps its frame rate. It waits until no other `resourcecompiler.exe` runs before each compile, and clears
  the old compiled materials first: their constant-colour textures carry the colour's hash in their name, so stale ones would ride along
  in the upload. Afterwards it moves vrad3's working files (about 50 MB) out of the addon folder and prints the log lines that mention an
  error or a warning.
- `preview.py` renders the Workshop preview (1280 x 720 JPEG) from `layout.json`: a CPU rasterizer in numpy and Pillow, so the GPU is
  left alone.

## Rebuild

```
python maps\gg_glasspire\build.py --novis --layout <scratch>\layout.json --log <scratch>\compile.log
python maps\gg_glasspire\preview.py <scratch>\layout.json <scratch>\preview.jpg
```

The map compile that shipped as build 1 (about 40 s, of which the GPU bake is 12 s and about 1 GB of video memory):

```
resourcecompiler.exe -threads 4 -fshallow -unbufferedio -i "<CS2>\content\csgo_addons\gg_glasspire\maps\gg_glasspire.vmap" -noassert -world -phys -breakpad -nop4 -retail -bakelighting -lightmapMaxResolution 1024 -lightmapVRadQuality 0
```

The materials are compiled first, because a map compile does not compile a missing child material; it fails each mesh with "illegal
resource name":

```
resourcecompiler.exe -threads 2 -f -nop4 -i "<CS2>\content\csgo_addons\gg_glasspire\materials\gg_glasspire\*.vmat"
```

**The Workshop upload is the whole addon folder** `game\csgo_addons\gg_glasspire`: `maps\gg_glasspire.vpk` and
`materials\gg_glasspire\`. The glass materials live outside the map's VPK; without them every pane is a missing material.

## Checks (build 1)

- **The compile:** 319 compiled, 0 failed. The log's only remarks: the usual `scripts/detail_prop_types.vdata_c` not found, Fossilize's
  pipeline-cache notice, and "No lightmap resolution volumes set".
- **The entity lump**, decompiled with Source2Viewer-CLI 20.1: 292 `func_breakable`, every one with `material 0` and `health 1`, and 292
  distinct models; 50 spawns, none over a pane; 52 `light_omni2` and 5 probe volumes, besides the sun, the sky and the exposure volume.
- **The map's VPK** holds the world, the world physics, the lightmaps (`irradiance`, `directional_irradiance`, `direct_light_shadows`)
  and the light probe atlas.
- **The size measure** found all 50 spawns standable and a walkable area of 3,725 m².

## Known gaps

- **No vis.** `-vis` ran over 4 minutes on this map without finishing, because the empty plaza inside the shell is most of the voxelised
  volume. The map draws whole. A tighter skybox shell, or no plaza, should make vis cheap.
- **No cubemaps baked.** CS2 bakes cubemaps in Hammer or in the running game (`-buildcubemaps`), and neither was used. The probe volumes
  carry the diffuse light, but specular reflections, the glass's included, fall back to whatever the engine has.
- **No lightmap resolution volume.** The plaza and the city share the 1024 atlas with the play space; a `toolslightmapres` box round the
  tower would sharpen the interior.
- **Broken glass stays broken** until the map reloads. A `point_template` on a timer could respawn it.
- **The break effect** of a `func_breakable` Glass brush in CS2 was read from `server.dll`'s strings, not seen in game: it plays
  `Breakable.Glass` and the pane disappears. Brush entities carry no break pieces, so expect few or no shards.
- **The light balance** - the 1800 lm lights and the 0.8-2.0 exposure range - is an educated guess: nobody had looked at it in a game
  client when build 1 shipped.
