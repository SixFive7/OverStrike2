The on-screen messages of Over Strike 2's HUD, as the message system has them: one line each, under its heading - who sees it and for
how long in brackets, then the surface it goes on. docshots.py holds its table of screenshots to this list (every message has a row) and
publish.py's gallery (docs/hud/messages.html) takes its headings and its words from it; a message NOT SHOWN has a row that says why.
A change to the message system changes this list, the table and the pictures together.

MESSAGES (50)

JOINING

- Welcome window, at every login [one player, needs an answer; until its X]: 7a the window, its own page (Centre-centre (needs an answer)).
- Join tagline [one player; 3 s]: a line on the window's page (Inside the window); also a line in chat.
- Welcome-back card [one player; 8 s]: a line on the window's page (Inside the window).
- Now-playing card, at each map's first spawn [everybody; 8 s]: 3c the map's own picture (Our card: centre, under the crosshair); also listed on the TAB board.
- "Erik joined with HUD v4_6_0" [everybody; 8 s]: 9a one line (CS2 plain centre text (PrintToCenter): white words, bottom centre, works without our HUD); also a line in chat; also listed on the TAB board.
